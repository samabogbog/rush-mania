# Lv50 VFX review

Reviewed six Low/High impact frames against Lv40 at the same camera, late expansion, continuous preview recordings and all six genuine learned-skill gameplay motion sheets. Lv50 introduces green wind blade wings, a gold shield fortress crown, a pink/white layered lotus, a lime leaf shield vault, a gold herbal arrow with white blossoms and a violet crossed hunting reticle with a purple arrow. Decorative radius grows from 7.8 to 9.2 units, with late echoes lasting up to 0.98 seconds. Local visual scope approved for user review. This does not certify a whole-game art score or production rollout.

Build and 46 checks passed on the final frozen runtime: 37 code checks and nine browser checks. All six authentic rank1 casts consumed MP, started cooldown, increased kills and cleared active effects on recovery. Model/page errors were zero. Preview frames are render-only; gameplay evidence uses real controls and prerequisites. Assets are original procedural geometry/materials. Active limits stay 40 Low / 96 High, allocated pool 160. Combat rules and online authority are unchanged.

Performance samples use Chromium151 ANGLE SwiftShader. Frozen impact versus idle measures render cost on this machine, not actual player FPS. Desktop60/mobile30 targets remain unverified. Large crowns can briefly obscure a target at close zoom; expansion and recovery reopen the view. Raw WebM recordings remain local and are ignored by Git. Release status is recorded separately in docs/RELEASE-LV50-TH.md.
